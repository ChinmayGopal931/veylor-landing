How it works

One key, split in two.

You hold half the key to the ZEC and the bridge holds the other. Whoever takes the USDC must reveal their half, which hands the ZEC to the other side.

The bridge goes first

Your app reads the locked USDC on Ethereum before any ZEC leaves.

No owner, no pause switch

Once a swap opens, nobody can change its terms or freeze it.

One side at a time

Deadlines give each side its own window, so no reveal can be raced.

The full protocol

The bridge goes quiet after I pay?

You claim the USDC without it.

I send too little?

The bridge cancels, which reveals its half, and your ZEC comes back.

My device is off for hours?

Once the bridge has confirmed, a relayer claims for you. Before that, the swap waits, then refunds.

The relayer misbehaves?

It can’t redirect anything: the swap can only pay the Railgun note you chose.

Railgun sends the payout back?

It returns to a vault only for that swap, and can be shielded again.
