A new kind of wallet

Shielded ZEC and shielded dollars, finally in one wallet.

Until now, shielded ZEC and shielded dollars lived in separate apps, and moving from one to the other meant going public. Veylor holds both, and swaps one for the other without either going public.

  • Native shielded ZEC. Zcash’s own shielded pool, private by default.
  • Shielded dollars. 0zkUSDC: USDC shielded on Ethereum, powered by Railgun.
  • A swap built in. Trustless and atomic. Nobody, including us, ever holds your money.
  • One recovery phrase. It restores everything.

The bridge locks first

Before you send anything, the bridge locks the dollars in a public contract, its source open on GitHub.

A key split in two

FAQ

Questions?

Anything else? Write to hello@veylor.cash.

What is Veylor?

A new kind of private wallet: native shielded ZEC and shielded USDC in one place, with a trustless, atomic swap built in. It’s self-custody, built on Vizor, and one recovery phrase restores everything.

Which platforms does Veylor run on?

Android, from Google Play or as an APK, and in beta on iPhone (TestFlight), Windows and Linux. The Mac app is coming soon. Every download is on the releases page.

Can I bring my Zcash from another wallet?

Yes. Import its recovery phrase into Veylor, or send your ZEC to a new Veylor wallet.

What is the shielded bridge?

An atomic swap between shielded ZEC and 0zkUSDC. The bridge locks its dollars in a public contract first. Your ZEC goes to a fresh shielded address whose key is split between you and the bridge, and revealing your half commits the dollars to your Railgun note before the bridge can take the ZEC. If a swap stalls, it ends in a refund. How it works

Why is it the first shielded bridge?

Every other way out of Zcash, through NEAR Intents or an exchange, unshields: your ZEC passes through a public address, its exact amount and time on show. Through Veylor’s bridge your ZEC never leaves the shielded pool, and the dollars land straight in Railgun. Compared with NEAR Intents

What is Railgun?

A non-custodial privacy system for Ethereum. Zero-knowledge proofs keep tokens at private 0zk addresses, where balances and transfers stay hidden. Learn more at railgun.org.

Why Railgun?

Dollars live on Ethereum, and Railgun shields them there without a custodian. It already runs on mainnet with its own wallets and users, so you can pay and get paid by anyone on Railgun, not only by Veylor. Veylor runs it inside the app, from the same recovery phrase.

Is the code open source?

Yes. The app is at github.com/ChinmayGopal931/vizor-fork and the bridge protocol at github.com/ChinmayGopal931/zecSwap, and every swap is public on the live contract. GitHub builds the Android, Linux and Windows releases from that code and signs a record of each build, which you can check on the releases page.

Has it been audited?

Not yet: no audit is published. Swaps run on mainnet with real funds, each one is capped, and the app uses a single bridge for now. Start small.

Who holds my keys?

You do. There is no account. Your recovery phrase stays in your phone’s secure storage, and nobody, including us, can recover it for you.
