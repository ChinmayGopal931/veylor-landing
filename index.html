Shielded dollars

Hold, send and receive shielded USDC.

Veylor is the first wallet to hold shielded ZEC and shielded dollars side by side. Your dollars are 0zkUSDC: USDC shielded in Railgun on Ethereum, where balances and transfers stay private. Move into dollars to hedge, and back into ZEC when you choose.

  • Send to anyone. Pay any Railgun 0zk address privately, or withdraw to any Ethereum address.
  • Receive from anyone. Share your 0zk address, or take USDC from any Ethereum account at your deposit address, and it shields itself into your balance.
  • No ETH for gas. A relayer sends the Ethereum transactions, for a small fee in USDC.
  • One seed. The Railgun wallet comes from your recovery phrase, so there's nothing new to back up.

The shielded bridge

Swap ZEC and dollars without unshielding.

Swapping ZEC through NEAR Intents starts with an unshield: your ZEC goes to a transparent deposit address, where its amount and time stay public for good, and the dollars that arrive on the other chain can be matched to it.

Veylor's bridge stays shielded the whole way. Your ZEC moves to a fresh shielded address, your dollars land shielded in Railgun, and Zcash sees one ordinary shielded transaction.

Trustless at every step

Verify every step. Trust no one.

A maker on the other side of each swap quotes the price and puts up the dollars. You never have to trust it, or us: the app checks the chain before every move, and an Ethereum contract enforces the deal.

The maker locks first

Before you send anything, the maker locks the dollars in the ZecSwap contract on Ethereum. It’s public, with its source verified on Etherscan.

Your app checks the lock

The app reads the lock back from Ethereum and only sends your ZEC if the amount, the deadlines and your payout note all match.

A key split in two

Your ZEC goes to a fresh shielded address whose key is split between you and the maker. Neither half can spend it alone.

Your claim comes first

To take the dollars, you reveal your half of the key, and the contract commits them to your Railgun note. Only then can the maker take the ZEC.

Every failure ends in a refund

If anything stalls, the deadlines settle it: the maker takes its dollars back, and that hands your app the key to move your ZEC home.

No owner, no off switch

The contract has no owner, admin key or pause switch. Anyone can be a maker on it, and anyone can send your signed requests.

Powered by Railgun

Your dollars stay shielded in Railgun.

Railgun is an on-chain, non-custodial privacy system for Ethereum and other EVM chains. It uses zero-knowledge cryptography to keep tokens at private 0zk addresses, and to send, receive and use them privately. Its code has no owner, and there's no single official wallet: many independent wallets use it.

Veylor runs a Railgun wallet inside the app, so your 0zkUSDC sits in Railgun's shielded pool without a separate wallet or seed. Learn more at railgun.org.

The pool Veylor uses 0zkUSDC USDC in Railgun's shielded pool on Ethereum, marked with Railgun's badge in the app.
