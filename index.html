The first shielded bridge for Zcash

Swap ZEC for private dollars without ever unshielding.

Veylor is a self-custody Zcash wallet that swaps shielded ZEC for USDC held privately in Railgun on Ethereum, and back. It's an atomic swap: nobody holds your funds, and every failure ends in a refund.

The problem

Swapping through NEAR Intents leaves a public trail.

Zcash wallets such as Zodl swap through NEAR Intents. To trade ZEC there, you first send it out of the shielded pool to a transparent deposit address. That send stays public on the Zcash blockchain for good: the exact amount, the exact time, and a known NEAR Intents deposit address. It's the kind of transaction chain-analysis firms flag. Soon after, about the same value in USDC arrives from a solver on the other chain, so amount and timing link the two.

Veylor's bridge never unshields. Your ZEC goes to a fresh shielded address whose key is split between you and the other side of the trade, so anyone reading Zcash sees one ordinary shielded transaction.

Trustless, not trusted

An atomic swap. Nobody holds your money.

On the other side of every swap is a maker, which quotes a price and puts up the dollars. You don't have to trust it, or us. A contract on Ethereum enforces the deal, and the maker can't touch your ZEC without your half of the key.

Nobody holds your funds

Your ZEC waits at a shielded address that needs both halves of a split key, and the dollars wait in the contract. At no point does anyone, including us, hold your money.

No operator to trust

Through NEAR Intents, your ZEC sits in someone else's transparent address mid-swap, where it can be frozen, seized or lost. Here there's nothing to freeze. The maker can refuse to quote you, but it can't touch a swap already in flight.

Every failure ends in a refund

A swap has only two endings, each fair to both sides. Either you claim your dollars and the maker takes the ZEC, or the maker takes its dollars back and your ZEC comes back to you.

Dollars and ZEC, one wallet

Hold private dollars next to your ZEC.

Zcash wallets hold ZEC. Veylor also holds 0zkUSDC, which is USDC kept privately in Railgun on Ethereum, in the same wallet. Move into dollars to hedge, and back into ZEC when you choose, without unshielding.

The Railgun wallet is built in and out of sight. There's no second wallet to set up, no second seed to back up, and no ETH to buy for gas: a relayer sends the Ethereum transactions. You see a dollar balance next to your ZEC, with all your assets in one place.

How it works

A split key and an atomic swap.

Your ZEC goes to a fresh shielded address whose key is split between you and the maker. The maker locks the dollars in an Ethereum contract first, and the only way to take them out is to reveal your half of the key, which hands the maker what it needs to take the ZEC. Either you get your dollars, or everything goes back where it came from.

Powered by Railgun

Your dollars stay private in Railgun.

Railgun is an on-chain, non-custodial privacy system for Ethereum and other EVM chains. It uses zero-knowledge cryptography to keep tokens at private 0zk addresses, and to send, receive and use them privately. Its code has no owner, and there's no single official wallet: many independent wallets use it.

Veylor runs a Railgun wallet inside the app, so your 0zkUSDC sits in Railgun's shielded pool without a separate wallet or seed. Learn more at railgun.org.

The pool Veylor uses 0zkUSDC USDC in Railgun's shielded pool on Ethereum, marked with Railgun's badge in the app.
