Trustless at every step

Verifiable at every step.

Don't trust the bridge, or us.

The bridge locks first

Before you send anything, the bridge locks the USDC in a public contract, its source open on GitHub.

A key split in two

Your ZEC goes to a fresh shielded address whose key is split between you and the bridge. Neither half can spend it alone.

Every failure ends in a refund

If a swap stalls, the bridge takes its USDC back, which hands your app the key to bring your ZEC home.

Powered by Railgun

Your USDC stays shielded in Railgun.

Railgun is a non-custodial privacy system for Ethereum. Zero-knowledge proofs keep tokens at private 0zk addresses.

Veylor runs one inside the app, so there's no second wallet or seed. Learn more at railgun.org.

The pool Veylor uses 0zkUSDC USDC in Railgun's shielded pool. It carries Railgun's badge in the app.

FAQ

Questions?

Write to hello@veylor.cash.

Never share your recovery phrase, keys or passcode, with us or anyone.

What is Veylor?

A self-custodial wallet for shielded ZEC and 0zkUSDC, with an atomic bridge between them built in. It’s built on Vizor.

Where does it run?

Android, from Google Play or as an APK, Windows and Linux, and in beta on iPhone. Mac is coming. Every download is on the releases page.

Can I bring my Zcash?

Yes. Import your recovery phrase, or send your ZEC to a new Veylor wallet.

What is 0zkUSDC?

USDC shielded in Railgun, a non-custodial privacy system on Ethereum. Zero-knowledge proofs hide its balances and transfers.

Why Railgun?

It shields USDC where USDC lives, on Ethereum, with no custodian. Anyone on Railgun can pay you, not only Veylor users.

Why is the bridge a first?

Every other way out of Zcash unshields: a public address, an exact amount, an exact time. Here your ZEC never leaves the shielded pool. Compare them

Has it been audited?

Not yet: no audit is published. Swaps run on mainnet with real funds, each one is capped, and the app uses a single bridge for now. Start small.

Who holds my keys?

You do. There is no account, and nobody, including us, can recover your phrase.
