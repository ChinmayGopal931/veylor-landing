VEYLOR

A private, self-custody Zcash wallet.

Your keys stay on your phone and there is no account to make. Transactions are shielded by default, so amounts, addresses and history stay private.

  • Send and receive ZEC, shielded by default
  • Private USD: dollars held privately on Ethereum through Railgun, converted to and from shielded ZEC
  • Swaps with other assets through NEAR Intents
  • Keystone and Ledger hardware wallets
  • Tor and Private queries

Veylor is in beta on iPhone through TestFlight.
