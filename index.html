Shielded dollars

Hold, send and receive shielded USDC.

0zkUSDC is USDC shielded in Railgun on Ethereum. Veylor is the first wallet to hold it beside shielded ZEC.

  • Send to anyone. Pay any 0zk or Ethereum address.
  • Receive from anyone. From any 0zk address or Ethereum account. Public USDC shields on arrival.
  • No ETH for gas. A relayer covers it for a small USDC fee.
  • One seed. Your Railgun wallet is derived from your Zcash recovery phrase.

The shielded bridge

Swap with dollars. Your ZEC stays shielded.

Veylor's bridge never unshields your ZEC.

Trustless at every step

Verifiable at every step.

Don't trust the maker, or us. Your app checks the chain before every move, and a public contract enforces the deal.

The maker locks first

Before you send anything, the maker locks the dollars in a public contract, its source open on GitHub.

Your app checks the lock

Your ZEC leaves only if the amount, deadlines and payout note on Ethereum all match.

A key split in two

Your ZEC goes to a fresh shielded address whose key is split between you and the maker. Neither half can spend it alone.

Your claim comes first

Revealing your half of the key commits the dollars to your Railgun note. Only then can the maker take the ZEC.

Every failure ends in a refund

If a swap stalls, the maker takes its dollars back, which hands your app the key to bring your ZEC home.

No owner, no off switch

The contract has no admin key. Anyone can be a maker or relay your signed requests.

Powered by Railgun

Your dollars stay shielded in Railgun.

Railgun is a non-custodial privacy system for Ethereum. Zero-knowledge proofs keep tokens at private 0zk addresses.

Veylor runs one inside the app, so there's no second wallet or seed. Learn more at railgun.org.

The pool Veylor uses 0zkUSDC USDC in Railgun's shielded pool. It carries Railgun's badge in the app.
